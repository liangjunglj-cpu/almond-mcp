# Construction spec register

Itemized source register for every parameter in `Constructionfiles/manifest.json`. Each value is traced to its section in **Francis D.K. Ching, *Building Construction Illustrated*, 4th ed. (Wiley, 2014)** ('BCI'); page numbers are PDF pages of that volume, matching [reference/bci_toc.txt](reference/bci_toc.txt). Values BCI defers to the building code are labeled *IBC-typical* and must be verified against the governing code.

Generated 2026-08-29 by `tools/build_spec_register.py` - regenerate after editing the manifest; do not edit this file by hand.

All rules of thumb are preliminary-sizing guidance for design plausibility, not a substitute for engineering analysis.

| spacing | element: joist; options 305 mm/405 mm/610 mm | BCI 4.26-4.32 |

**Elements:**

- `joist` - 38 mm ('2x') dimension lumber on edge; 2x6 spans to about 3.0 m, 2x12 to about 5.5 m depending on species, grade and load.
- `rim_joist` - Rim joist or header closes and laterally supports joist ends; double end joists under parallel partitions.
- `subfloor` - Wood panel sheathing ties and stabilizes joists against twisting and buckling, and can act as a structural diaphragm.
- `support` - Joists bear on wood/steel beams or loadbearing stud walls; lateral support required at joist ends.

**Construction notes:**

- Spacing is chosen against sheathing span rating and load magnitude.
- Openings and cantilevers need doubled trimmers and headers.
- Combustible light framing: fire rating comes from the finish assembly.
